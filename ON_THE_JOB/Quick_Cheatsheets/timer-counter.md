# Cheatsheet — Timers & counters

## TIMER members
`.PRE` `.ACC` `.EN` `.TT` `.DN` — PRE in **ms** typically

## Pick one
| Need | Use |
|------|-----|
| Delay after condition true | TON |
| Keep on after condition false | TOF |
| Totalize time | RTO + RES |

## COUNTER
Count **edges** with ONS for field sensors that stay on.
