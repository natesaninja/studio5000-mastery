# Teach-back — Phase 1 Foundation

**Time box:** 15–20 min  
**Prerequisite:** P1 complete, cumulative quiz ≥ 85%

---

## Part A — Explain (10 min)

Teach an apprentice:

1. What a PLC **scan** is (input image → logic → output image).  
2. Why the PLC is **not** a voltmeter.  
3. Task vs Program vs Routine using a packaging line.  
4. What a **tag** and an **alias** are.  
5. XIC, XIO, OTE and the **NC stop trap**.  
6. How a **seal-in** start/stop maps to hardwired 3-wire control.  
7. What a **force** is and why leaving one is like leaving a jumper.  
8. Your **first five checks** when a conveyor won’t start.

## Part B — Challenge questions (5 min)

Answer closed-notes:

1. CPU RUN good, all inputs false — what power do you meter first?  
2. `Run_Cmd` true, DO LED off — what family of causes?  
3. Download vs upload direction?  
4. Why put guard in the seal-in path?  
5. Name the six troubleshooting buckets.

## Part C — Demo (optional but strong)

Live or Emulate: start/stop conveyor; show monitor tags; show empty force table.
