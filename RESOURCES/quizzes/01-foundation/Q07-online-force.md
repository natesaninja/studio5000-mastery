# Quiz 1.7 — Online, download, force

**Closed notes.** 10 questions. Pass ≥ 8/10.

1. Download direction: PC → controller or controller → PC?  
2. Upload risk in one line?  
3. Force is analogous to what field action?  
4. List four items a force log must include.  
5. When must you refuse a download?  
6. Does green rung animation guarantee 24 V at the starter coil?  
7. First checks if controller not in communications browser?  
8. Why confirm controller **name/identity** before download?  
9. After forcing Start for a test, what is mandatory?  
10. Who “owns” the machine when you are online making changes?

---

## Answers

1. **PC → controller**.  
2. Overwriting PC project edits with controller content.  
3. Jumper / taped contact / forced override.  
4. Tag; value; reason; time; who (name); and removal status.  
5. Wrong controller/path, no authorization, unknown revision impact, unsafe state.  
6. **No.**  
7. Power, cable, driver, IP/subnet, switch, wrong network.  
8. Avoid downloading to the neighbor cell/controller.  
9. **Remove the force** and verify real device control; confirm force tables empty.  
10. Operations/site authority — you need authorization; you don’t privately own production equipment.
